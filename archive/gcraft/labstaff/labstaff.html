JESSE, Lab Mascot

Née Commera (w. 22 October 1989, s. Crimson Falcon, d. Sports Affair). Jesse retired to Austin after a successful racing career in Wisconsin and Florida. She now makes her home in Lafayette, Colorado.  For information about adopting ex-racing greyhounds contact Greyhound Pets of America at (800) 366-1472. Adoption information is also available at Greyhound Adoption Group Pages Hall of Fame, Greyhound Pets of America California Adoption Center, and USA Defenders of Greyhounds .
