VARIABLES IN FILE EPIDEMIC.DBF: DATA ON CHOLERA EPIDEMIC IN PERU, 1991

POTABLEWAT: NUMBER OF PEOPLE IN RURAL AREAS WITH ACCESS TO CLEAN WATER
