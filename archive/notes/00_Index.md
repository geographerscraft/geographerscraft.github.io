## Content Summary by Topic

### Technical Foundations (Peter Dana)

These three modules form the core technical foundation of geospatial technologies:

1. **GPS Overview** - The most popular module (1,241 captures!), covering satellite positioning, signals, errors, and applications
2. **Map Projections** - Comprehensive coverage of projection types and their mathematical foundations
3. **Coordinate Systems** - Datums, reference frames, UTM, State Plane, and global systems

### GIS Concepts (Foote & Lynch)

4. **GIS Introduction** - Foundational understanding of GIS as an integrating technology

### Professional Practice (Mixed Authors)

5. **Managing Error** (Foote & Huebner) - Data quality, accuracy, precision, and uncertainty
6. **Legal Issues** (Lynch & Foote) - FOIA, privacy, liability, and copyright
7. **Project Lifecycle** (Foote & Crum) - SDLC, requirements, planning, and implementation
