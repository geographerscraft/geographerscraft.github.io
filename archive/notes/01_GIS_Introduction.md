# Geographic Information Systems as an Integrating Technology

## Context, Concepts, and Definitions

**Authors:** Kenneth E. Foote and Margaret Lynch  
**Affiliation:** Department of Geography, University of Texas at Austin  
**Original Date:** 1995  
**Last Archived:** August 4, 2002 (University of Colorado Boulder)  
**Source:** The Geographer's Craft Project

---

## Overview

This module serves as the primary introductory text for "The Geographer's Craft" project. It establishes GIS not just as a piece of software, but as an integrating technology that bridges traditional geographic methods with modern digital tools.
