## Overview

This page provides a comprehensive technical overview of the Global Positioning System (GPS). It served as a foundational resource for GIS and GPS education in the late 1990s and early 2000s, and remains one of the most frequently cited educational resources on GPS.

---

## Major Sections & Topics Covered

### 1. U.S. Department of Defense Satellite Navigation System

- Introduction to the system's development and management

### 2. GPS Positioning Services

- **Standard Positioning Service (SPS)** - Civilian service
- **Precise Positioning Service (PPS)** - Military service
- As specified in the Federal Radionavigation Plan

### 3. GPS Satellite Signals

- L1 Frequency (1575.42 MHz)
- L2 Frequency (1227.60 MHz)
- C/A Code (Coarse Acquisition)
- P-Code (Precise)

### 4. GPS Data

- Navigation Message structure
- Data frames and subframes
- Ephemeris data
- Almanac data

### 5. Position and Time from GPS

- Pseudorange measurements
- Carrier-phase measurements
- Time transfer

### 6. GPS Error Sources

- Ionospheric delays
- Tropospheric delays
- Multipath effects
- Clock errors
- Receiver noise

### 7. Geometric Dilution of Precision (GDOP)

- **PDOP** - Position Dilution of Precision
- **HDOP** - Horizontal Dilution of Precision
- **VDOP** - Vertical Dilution of Precision
- **TDOP** - Time Dilution of Precision

### 8. Differential GPS Techniques

- DGPS (Differential GPS)
- Real-Time Kinematic (RTK)
- Accuracy improvements

### 9. Related Documents

- Technical specifications
- Federal standards
- Manuals

### 10. Related Links

- External resources (many now archived)

### 11. Reference List

- Academic and technical citations
