## Overview

Map projections are mathematical transformations that convert spherical coordinates (latitude/longitude) to a planar surface. This page provides a comprehensive classification of map projections based on the developable surface used.

---

## Major Sections & Projection Types

### 1. Introduction

- Definition of map projections
- Transformation from spherical to planar coordinates

### 2. Cylindrical Projections

**Cases:** Tangent, Secant, Transverse, Oblique

**Specific Projections:**

- Cylindrical Equal-Area
  - Behrmann
  - Gall's Stereographic
  - Peters
- **Mercator**
- Miller Cylindrical
- Oblique Mercator
- Transverse Mercator
- **Universal Transverse Mercator (UTM)**

### 3. Pseudocylindrical Projections

**Specific Projections:**

- Mollweide
- Eckert IV
- Eckert VI
- **Robinson**
- Sinusoidal Equal-Area

### 4. Conic Projections

**Cases:** Tangent (one standard parallel), Secant (two standard parallels)

**Specific Projections:**

- **Albers Equal-Area Conic**
- Equidistant Conic
- **Lambert Conformal Conic**
- Polyconic

### 5. Azimuthal Projections

**Aspects:** Polar, Equatorial, Oblique

**Specific Projections:**

- Azimuthal Equidistant
- Lambert Azimuthal Equal Area
- **Orthographic**
- **Stereographic**

### 6. Miscellaneous Projections

**Unprojected Maps:**

- Simple Latitude and Longitude plots (Plate Carrée)

**Specialized:**

- Texas State-Wide Projection (Lambert Conformal Conic for Texas)
- Space Oblique Mercator (for satellite imagery)
