# Legal Issues Relating to GIS

**Authors:** Margaret Lynch and Kenneth E. Foote  
**Last Revised:** March 27, 2000  
**Source:** The Geographer's Craft Project

---

## Overview

This module examines the legal perspectives surrounding Geographic Information Systems, including public access rights, privacy concerns, liability issues, and copyright considerations.

---

## Major Sections and Topics Covered

### 1. Public Access

**Freedom of Information Act (FOIA):**

- Federal law enabling public access to government records
- Exemptions and limitations

**State Open-Records Acts:**

- Varying state-level public access laws
- Requirements for data availability

**Cost Considerations:**

- Tension between public access rights and data creation costs
- Questions of who bears distribution costs

### 2. Liability

**Legal Risks for GIS Practitioners:**

- Liability for **simple mistakes** in data or analysis
- Liability for **flawed policy** results based on GIS analysis
- **Unintended or inappropriate use** of data by third parties

**Key Concern:**

- Users often assume GIS data is "error-free"
- Need for appropriate disclaimers and documentation

### 3. Privacy

**Core Conflicts:**

- Balance between public information access and individual privacy protection
- Location data sensitivity

**Historical Context (1990s):**

- **Clipper Chip** debates
- **Encryption (PGP)** controversies
- Early discussions of digital privacy

### 4. GIS Data as Evidence

- Standards for using digital spatial data in court
- Challenges of digital evidence authentication
- Chain of custody for spatial data

### 5. Copyrights

**U.S. Federal Government Policy:**

- Data generally in public domain
- No copyright on federal works

**International Comparison:**

- **Crown Copyright** in Great Britain
- Different international approaches to government data ownership

### 6. Readings and Resources

- Bibliography of supplemental readings
- "Internet Starting Points" for legal research in GIS
- Information technology law resources

### 7. Examination and Study Questions

- Pedagogical tool for students
- Test understanding of legal concepts
