# 📖 Detailed Background & Context: The Geographer's Craft

## 1. Historical Background
- **Creation (1990s):** Created by **Dr. Ken Foote** at UT Austin as an innovative web-based curriculum for spatial methods, cartography, and GIS.
- **Peter Dana's Modules:** Dr. Peter H. Dana authored the widely cited *Global Positioning System Overview* and *Coordinate Systems & Datums* modules, which became standard reading across university geography programs nationwide.
- **Dr. Sounny's Connection:** Dr. Moulay Anwar Sounny-Slitine served as a Teaching Assistant (TA) under Peter Dana for *The Geographer's Craft* at UT Austin. This experience heavily shaped Dr. Sounny's teaching career across Southwestern University, University of Florida, International Space University (ISU), and Texas Southern University (TSU).

## 2. Migration History
- **1990s – 2013:** Hosted on UT Austin servers (`www.utexas.edu`).
- **Relative Link Revision:** Lance Christian (while at UT Austin) updated all HTML links to relative paths so the site could be moved without breaking links.
- **2013 – 2024:** Hosted at the University of Connecticut by Ken Foote (`uconn.edu`).
- **2024 Offline Status:** UConn implemented strict university-wide ADA compliance policies. Because the legacy 1990s HTML files required accessibility remediation before public server hosting, the files were temporarily taken offline. Ken Foote retained a full `.zip` backup.

## 3. 2026 Re-Hosting Initiative
In January 2026, Dr. Sounny re-engaged Ken Foote and Peter Dana to migrate the archive to GitHub Pages. Both authors approved open-sourcing the project.
In August 2026, Dr. Sounny prepared an email outreach to request the `.zip` backup file and offer GitHub Organization co-ownership to Ken Foote and Peter Dana.
