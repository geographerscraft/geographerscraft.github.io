Reference Ellipsoid, Equatorial Radius (a) , Reciprocal Flattening (1/f), Delta a, Delta f (*10^4)

Delta parameters are with respect to WGS-84 Parameters for conversion from WGS-84 to the specified datum

Parameter Delta a is the WGS-84 Equatorial radius minus the specified datum Equatorial radius in meters

Parameter Delta f is the WGS-84 flattening minus the specified datum flattening multiplied by 10^4

  • WGS-84 Equatorial Radius (a) = 6378137.0
  • WGS-84 Flattening (f) = 1/298.257223563

Source

Defense Mapping Agency. 1987b. DMA Technical Report: Supplement to Department of Defense World Geodetic System 1984 Technical Report. Part I and II. Washington, DC: Defense Mapping Agency
