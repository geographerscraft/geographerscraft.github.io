## VI. Author Biographies

### Kenneth E. Foote, Ph.D.

- **Current Position:** Professor, University of Connecticut
- **Former Positions:** University of Texas at Austin, University of Colorado Boulder
- **Expertise:** GIScience education, historical geography, landscape studies
- **Notable Works:** _Shadowed Ground: America's Landscapes of Violence and Tragedy_, _Re-reading Cultural Geography_ (co-editor)
- **Recognition:** UCGIS Fellow

### Peter H. Dana

- **Affiliation:** Consultant, Former University of Texas at Austin
- **Expertise:** GPS, electronic navigation, precise positioning, coordinate systems
- **Professional Services:** Expert witness in GPS and coordinate systems patent litigation
