## Key Lecture Notes (Direct Archive Links)

### GIS Introduction

**Title:** "Geographic Information Systems as an Integrating Technology: Context, Concepts, and Definitions"
**Authors:** Kenneth E. Foote and Margaret Lynch
**Archive:** `https://web.archive.org/web/20020806045349/http://www.colorado.edu/geography/gcraft/notes/intro/intro_f.html`

**Topics Covered:**

- Definitions of cartography, CAD, photogrammetry, remote sensing
- Spatial statistics and GIS
- Technological innovation in geography
- GIS as an integrating technology

---

### GPS Overview

**Title:** "The Global Positioning System (GPS)"
**Author:** Peter H. Dana
**Archive:** `https://web.archive.org/web/20020806045349/http://www.colorado.edu/geography/gcraft/notes/gps/gps_f.html`

**Topics Covered:**

- GPS components and constellation
- Position determination
- Time transfer
- Error sources
- Real-time applications

---

### Map Projections

**Title:** "Map Projection Overview"
**Author:** Peter H. Dana
**Archive:** `https://web.archive.org/web/20020806045349/http://www.colorado.edu/geography/gcraft/notes/mapproj/mapproj_f.html`

**Topics Covered:**

- Mathematical transformation models
- Cylindrical, conic, azimuthal projections
- Distortion types (shape, distance, direction, area)
- Common projection systems

## Retrieval Notes

### How to Access Archived Content

1. **Navigate to:** `https://web.archive.org`
2. **Enter URL:** `http://www.colorado.edu/geography/gcraft/contents.html`
3. **Select date:** Choose from available captures (best captures from 2002)
4. **Browse structure:** Navigate through the archived site

### Download Considerations

- Some pages may have broken internal links
- Images and graphics may not be fully preserved
- Consider downloading entire site sections for local hosting
- Use tools like `wget` with Wayback Machine compatibility
