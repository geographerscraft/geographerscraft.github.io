Cholera in Peru Grading Rubric

 

1) Analysis and Explanation of Trends (50 points)

How well does the report address the following questions:

· What factors seem to account for the pattern of cholera infection in 1991?
· What factors seem to account for the patterns of fatality?

2) Quality of Maps (25 points)

The following points will be considered:

· Overall layout and composition
· Composition of map elements
· Use of text and annotations
 

3) Quality of Web Presentation (25 points)

The following issues will be considered:

· Does the report offer a clear, concisely organized homepage?
· Are supporting pages well organized and easy to navigate?
· Do all links work?
