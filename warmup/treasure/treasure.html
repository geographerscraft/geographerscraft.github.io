Treasure Hunt

The World-Wide-Web (WWW) is a wonderful source of information, with everything from census statistics to on-line art exhibits. However, navigating the Web can be a time-consuming, and daunting, task. It may be simplified by planning searches before you begin and making use of one of the many search engines which are available.

Each of the following items is on-line in the WWW or a gopher server connected to the Web. They are ranked according to how difficult they are to locate, one asterisk (*) being the easiest and three (***) the most difficult. Some are "right on top" of the web, others are buried several layers down. How many can you find?
